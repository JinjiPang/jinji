Philosophy

An atheist who half-believes in destiny. "一个热爱艺术的科研学渣" — a science person who loves art.

My name is Jinji — people here usually call me Kimki. Growing up as an only child, I learned to be independent early: navigating decisions on my own, finding my way home from adventures in the mountains (literally). That spirit of adventure has never left me.

我叫逄金吉，这里的朋友通常叫我Kimki。从小一个人长大，学会了自己做决定，也学会了凭着自己的"机智"从山上找到回家的路。

I've always been someone who does a little bit of everything — sports, cooking, calligraphy, music. A generalist who never quite mastered anything, and somehow at peace with that. What I do know is that art — especially traditional Chinese art — moves me in ways science cannot fully explain.

我是个什么都喜欢但什么都不精的人——运动、做菜、书法、音乐，样样都沾一点。我对自己的评价是："一个热爱艺术的科研学渣。"尤其喜欢中国传统艺术，比如民乐和书法。

This site is built with blogdown (an R package by Yihui Xie) and serves as my personal journal — a place for travel diaries, reading notes, and the random thoughts that find their way onto a page.

这个网站用R的blogdown包搭建，是我的私人角落，用来记录旅行、读书和生活里的各种碎碎念。
